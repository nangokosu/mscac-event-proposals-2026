## **MScAC Initiative Budget Proposal**

**Event:** Canada's Wonderland Trip
**Date:** Saturday, October 10, 2026, 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM
**Venue:** Canada's Wonderland
**Estimated Attendance:** 28 students

---

### **Purpose**

This outing strengthens cohort bonds through shared experiences outside the classroom, creating memories and friendships that sustain the program community. An amusement park provides an ideal setting for this—students can enjoy rides together, explore at their own pace, and naturally socialize throughout the day. Final attendance puts 28 students: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1kgueoQNEyWqDuGMxy_rFEMqkapfdYC9HOiG5Gv7c1to/edit?gid=835449337#gid=835449337

Note that this will only cover admissions and rides. Students must figure out their own transportation and food arrangements.

### **Event Components**

**Arrival & Park Entry (11:30 AM):**

* Group meets at the designated meeting point; Khai Nguyen distributes pre-purchased group admission tickets to all attendees.
* Each student receives their ticket and enters the park together, establishing a shared starting point before exploring independently.

**Full-Day Park Exploration (11:30 AM – 4:30 PM):**

* Students explore Canada's Wonderland at their own pace, enjoying rides, attractions, and games across the park.
* The group remains flexible throughout the day, allowing students to self-organize into smaller groups or ride individually based on preference and ride comfort levels.
* Attendees are responsible for arranging their own meals and refreshments at the park's dining facilities.

**Departure & Group Debrief (4:30 PM – 5:00 PM):**

* Group reconvenes at a designated meeting location in the park to ensure all attendees are accounted for before heading out.
* Students arrange their own transportation home.
