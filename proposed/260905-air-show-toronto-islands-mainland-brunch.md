## **MScAC Initiative Budget Proposal**

**Event:** MScAC Cohort Day Trip — Canadian International Air Show at the Toronto Islands (mainland brunch option)
**Date:** Saturday, September 5, 2026, 9:00 AM to 5:15 PM
**Venue:** Impact Kitchen, 88 Queens Quay W (RBC WaterPark Place) for brunch; Centre Island Pier / Manitou (Centreville) Beach, Toronto Islands for viewing; round-trip ferry from the Jack Layton Ferry Terminal, 9 Queens Quay W
**Estimated Attendance:** 10 students

---

### **Purpose**

A full-day outing at the start of the term gives the cohort a relaxed, shared experience to bond over before coursework intensifies, trading the classroom for a day outdoors together. An air show on the Islands suits that goal well: it is a low-cost, casual, all-ages format with no structured programming to manage, so the group can eat, travel, and watch the show at its own pace while staying together. The 2026 show is billed as the Canadian Forces Snowbirds' final Canadian International Air Show appearance, which drew clear interest when the idea was floated in the cohort Discord interest poll, making this a fitting occasion for the cohort's first trip of the year.

### **Event Components**

This version keeps the same date and viewing plan as the primary proposal; the only difference is the meal — a group brunch on the mainland before the ferry rather than lunch on the island. Eating first lets the group board an earlier ferry and catch the full show from the pre-show water display onward.

**Group Brunch at Impact Kitchen (9:00 AM – 10:15 AM):**

* The group meets at Impact Kitchen, 88 Queens Quay W, about a 5-minute walk west of the ferry terminal along the Queens Quay promenade; it opens at 9:00 AM on Saturdays and Sundays.
* Impact Kitchen is a nutrition-focused, 100% gluten-free all-day café with the strongest vegetarian menu of the near-terminal options — power bowls, Kale Caesar, Zesty Mexican bowl, superfood benny, protein pancakes, smoothies — plus extensive vegan and gluten-free choices, which covers the several vegetarian cohort members well.
* Service is counter / fast-casual; Tuan Khai Nguyen, the trip organizer, places the group order and pays once so the whole meal lands on a single itemized receipt, and calls ahead so the kitchen is ready for a 10-person order.
* All beverages purchased are non-alcoholic (soft drinks, juice, water, smoothies); no alcohol will be bought or reimbursed.
* If the trip date were to move to Labour Day Monday, Impact Kitchen's holiday hours must be confirmed; otherwise the group falls back to The Mizzen at the Westin Harbour Castle (1 Harbour Square, about 4 minutes from the terminal, breakfast 8:00–11:00 AM daily with guaranteed holiday hours and a harbour view, roughly $43 per head expected).

**Travel & Ferry Crossing (10:20 AM – 11:15 AM):**

* The group walks from Impact Kitchen to the Jack Layton Ferry Terminal, with Tuan Khai holding the pre-purchased round-trip tickets for the whole group.
* Online ticket holders use the express boarding line, which matters on Labour Day weekend when the terminal is at its busiest.
* The group takes roughly the 10:45 AM Centre Island ferry; the crossing takes about 15 minutes.

**Air Show Viewing (11:30 AM – 3:40 PM):**

* From the Centre Island dock it is a 5–10 minute walk to the Centre Island Pier / Manitou Beach on the south shore.
* The group is in place for the pre-show water display from around 11:30 AM and stays through the full show (12:00 PM to 3:40 PM), including the Snowbirds finale at approximately 3:15–3:40 PM — nothing missed.
* Shared supplies (sunscreen, a first-aid kit, wet wipes, garbage bags) are carried by Tuan Khai; the group packs out its own trash.

**Return (4:00 PM – 5:15 PM):**

* The group walks back to the Centre Island dock and takes the ferry to the mainland.
* Departing after 4:00 PM lets the immediate post-show ferry surge clear before the group boards.

### **Budget Breakdown**

| Item Description | Quantity | Estimated Cost |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Brunch:** Group breakfast at Impact Kitchen (88 Queens Quay W) — one breakfast main + one non-alcoholic drink + a side or smoothie per person, incl. 13% HST; paid as one itemized bill | For 10 people | $450 |
| **Ferry:** Round-trip Toronto Island ferry tickets (Centre Island route), City of Toronto, purchased online in advance; budgeted at full adult fare $9.57/person (10% group discount on 10+ tickets applied if headcount holds) | 10 | $95 |
| **Supplies:** Sunscreen, shared first-aid kit, wet wipes, garbage bags | Bulk | $15 |

The total sits well below the $800–$875 single-event precedent because the group is small (10) and there is no venue rental or equipment cost — the only non-food line is the ferry. Food works out to about $45 per head, which is slightly above the $35–40 per-head norm; this reflects Impact Kitchen's whole-foods, gluten-free menu and the strong vegetarian coverage it provides. The primary proposal's island lunch stays within the norm at about $40 per head and comes in $50 lower overall, so this option is put forward as the alternative rather than the default.

### **RSVP Plan**

* **Interest Polling:** Tuan Khai Nguyen gauged interest with a poll in the cohort Discord server, where the trip drew enough support to move forward.
* **Preference Tracking:** Final RSVPs are collected on a Partiful event page, which confirms the headcount for ticket and food quantities.
* **Dietary Needs:** The Partiful page includes a dietary-restrictions question; the vegetarian count from those responses is confirmed with Impact Kitchen when the group order is booked, and vegetarian mains are reserved for those attendees.
* **Accessibility:** The Partiful page also asks about mobility or accessibility needs. Impact Kitchen is street-level and steps from the terminal, and both the Toronto Island ferry and the Centre Island paths are wheelchair accessible with short walking legs (about 5 minutes terminal to café, 5 to 10 minutes dock to viewing spot); Tuan Khai will adjust the route and pace or arrange the accessible ferry loading as needed.

### **Other Next Steps**

* **Ferry Tickets:** Tuan Khai Nguyen buys all round-trip tickets online in a single transaction at least a week before the trip, so the group qualifies for the 10% discount on 10+ tickets and for express boarding.
* **Venue:** Call Impact Kitchen ahead to confirm it can take a 10-person order on the day and to confirm the vegetarian mains; if the date shifts to Labour Day Monday, confirm holiday hours or switch to The Mizzen.
* **Reporting:** Submit all itemized receipts, final attendee list, and invitation for reimbursement. (ESSENTIAL)
