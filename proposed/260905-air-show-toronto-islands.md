## **MScAC Initiative Budget Proposal**

**Event:** MScAC Cohort Day Trip — Canadian International Air Show at the Toronto Islands
**Date:** Saturday, September 5, 2026, 10:00 AM to 5:15 PM
**Venue:** Centre Island Pier / Manitou (Centreville) Beach, Toronto Islands (viewing area); round-trip ferry from the Jack Layton Ferry Terminal, 9 Queens Quay W
**Estimated Attendance:** 10 students

---

### **Purpose**

A full-day outing at the start of the term gives the cohort a relaxed, shared experience to bond over before coursework intensifies, trading the classroom for a day outdoors together. An air show on the Islands suits that goal well: it is a low-cost, casual, all-ages format with no structured programming to manage, so the group can travel, eat, and watch the show at its own pace while staying together off-campus. The 2026 show is billed as the Canadian Forces Snowbirds' final Canadian International Air Show appearance, which drew clear interest when the idea was floated in the cohort Discord interest poll, making this a fitting occasion for the cohort's first trip of the year.

### **Event Components**

**Travel & Ferry Crossing (10:00 AM – 11:00 AM):**

* The group meets at the Jack Layton Ferry Terminal, 9 Queens Quay W, with Tuan Khai Nguyen, the trip organizer, holding the pre-purchased round-trip tickets for the whole group.
* Online ticket holders use the express boarding line, which matters on Labour Day weekend when the terminal is at its busiest.
* The crossing to Centre Island takes roughly 15 minutes.

**Early Lunch at Carousel Café (11:10 AM – 11:50 AM):**

* From the Centre Island dock it is about a 7-minute walk to the Carousel Café inside Centreville, which opens at 11:00 AM — the earliest full food service on the island.
* Service is counter / fast-casual with patio seating; Tuan Khai places the group order and pays in a single transaction so the whole meal lands on one itemized receipt.
* Several cohort members are vegetarian; the café has confirmed vegetarian options (veggie burger, margherita / vegetarian flatbread, grilled cheese, salads, fries), and Tuan Khai will confirm vegetarian mains with the venue when booking the group order.
* All beverages purchased are non-alcoholic (soft drinks, juice, water); the café is licensed but no alcohol will be bought or reimbursed.

**Air Show Viewing (12:00 PM – 3:40 PM):**

* From Centreville it is about a 5-minute walk south to the Centre Island Pier / Manitou Beach on the south shore, an open viewing spot roughly 5 minutes from the ferry dock.
* A pre-show water display begins around 11:30 AM, with the main show running 12:00 PM to 3:40 PM.
* The group stays for the full program, including the Snowbirds finale at approximately 3:15–3:40 PM.
* Shared supplies (sunscreen, a first-aid kit, wet wipes, garbage bags) are carried by Tuan Khai; the group packs out its own trash.

**Return (4:00 PM – 5:15 PM):**

* The group walks back to the Centre Island dock and takes the ferry to the mainland.
* Departing after 4:00 PM lets the immediate post-show ferry surge clear before the group boards.

| :---- | :---- | :---- |

| **Ferry:** Round-trip Toronto Island ferry tickets (Centre Island route), City of Toronto, purchased online in advance; budgeted at full adult fare $9.57/person (10% group discount on 10+ tickets applied if headcount holds) | 10 | $95 |
| **Supplies:** Sunscreen, shared first-aid kit, wet wipes, garbage bags | Bulk | $15 |

The total sits well below the $800–$875 single-event precedent because the group is small (10) and there is no venue rental or equipment cost — the only non-food line is the ferry. Food works out to about $40 per head, near the top of the $35–40 per-head norm but within it.

### **RSVP Plan**

* **Interest Polling:** Tuan Khai Nguyen gauged interest with a poll in the cohort Discord server, where the trip drew enough support to move forward.
* **Preference Tracking:** Final RSVPs are collected on a Partiful event page, which confirms the headcount for ticket and food quantities.
* **Dietary Needs:** The Partiful page includes a dietary-restrictions question; the vegetarian count from those responses is confirmed with the Carousel Café when the group order is booked, and vegetarian mains are reserved for those attendees.
* **Accessibility:** The Partiful page also asks about mobility or accessibility needs. Both the Toronto Island ferry and the Centre Island paths are wheelchair accessible, and the walking legs are short (about 7 minutes from the dock to the café, 5 minutes to the viewing spot); Tuan Khai will adjust the route and pace or arrange the accessible ferry loading as needed.
