## **MScAC Initiative Budget Proposal**

**Event:** MScAC Cohort Day Trip — Toronto Islands
**Date:** Friday, September 4, 2026, 11:00 AM to 5:30 PM
**Venue:** Toronto Islands (Ward's Island and Centre Island); lunch at The Riviera ~ Ward's Island Kitchen, 102 Lakeshore Ave, Ward's Island; round-trip ferry from the Jack Layton Ferry Terminal, 9 Queens Quay W
**Estimated Attendance:** 15–20 students

---

### **Purpose**

A low-structure day off-campus early in the term gives the cohort time to decompress and bond before coursework ramps up. The islands suit this because the day has no schedule to manage — the group wanders the beaches, boardwalk, and gardens at its own pace and regroups for a shared meal. A poll in the cohort Discord server drew 19 students saying yes to the Friday date.

### **Event Components**

**Travel & Ferry Crossing (11:00 AM – 11:45 AM):**

* The group meets at the Jack Layton Ferry Terminal, 9 Queens Quay W.
* Tuan Khai Nguyen holds the pre-purchased Ward's Island round-trip tickets for the whole group; online ticket holders use the express boarding line.
* The crossing to Ward's Island takes about 15 minutes.

**Morning Wander — Ward's Island (11:45 AM – 1:15 PM):**

* The group explores the Ward's Island village lanes, the boardwalk, and the beach.
* It drifts west along the shared path toward Centre Island at its own pace.

**Lunch at The Riviera ~ Ward's Island Kitchen (1:15 PM – 2:45 PM):**

* The restaurant sits about a 7-minute walk from the Ward's Island dock.
* Tuan Khai arranges a curated group menu for 15–20 people with the restaurant by email or phone in advance, places the group order on the day, and settles one itemized bill on one card.
* Several cohort members are vegetarian and some are vegan, and The Riviera's kitchen makes vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free dishes (gluten-free vegan lasagna, Beyond Meat burger, wood-oven vegetable pizza, Buddha bowl); the booking confirms the vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free counts and names their substitutions on the group menu.
* All beverages purchased are non-alcoholic; the restaurant is licensed, but no alcohol is bought or reimbursed.

**Afternoon Exploring — Centre Island (2:45 PM – 4:45 PM):**

* The group takes in the Avenue of the Islands, the formal gardens, the pier, and the Franklin Children's Garden.
* The group may continue to Hanlan's Point beach if time allows.

**Return (4:45 PM – 5:30 PM):**

* The group takes the ferry back to the mainland from the Ward's Island or Centre Island dock.

### **Budget Breakdown**

| Item Description | Quantity | Estimated Cost |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Lunch:** Curated group menu at The Riviera ~ Ward's Island Kitchen (102 Lakeshore Ave, Ward's Island) — one main + one non-alcoholic drink per person, incl. 13% HST and ~20% gratuity; one reservation, paid as one itemized bill | For 20 people | $800 |
| **Ferry:** Round-trip Toronto Island ferry tickets (Ward's Island route), City of Toronto, purchased online in advance; budgeted at the full adult fare of $9.57/person as a buffer, with the 10% group discount on 10+ tickets bringing the actual cost lower | 20 | $190 |

The budget is built for the top of the 15–20 range; at 15 attendees the expected spend is about $745. Food works out to about $40 per head, at the top of the $35–40 per-head norm but within it — The Riviera is the only island venue able to serve genuine vegan mains, which the cohort requires. The outing needs no equipment or materials, and there is no venue-rental cost. The total of $990 is above the $800–$875 single-event precedent only because of the ferry transport line, a cost category the on-campus precedents never carried; the $800 food spend on its own sits within the precedent band.
