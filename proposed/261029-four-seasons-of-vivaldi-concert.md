## **MScAC Initiative Budget Proposal**

**Event:** MScAC Cohort Outing — "The Four Seasons of Vivaldi" at Roy Thomson Hall
**Date:** Thursday, October 29, 2026, 7:00 PM to 10:30 PM
**Venue:** Roy Thomson Hall, 60 Simcoe Street, Toronto (reserved Balcony-level seating)
**Estimated Attendance:** 12–14 students

---

### **Purpose**

A shared evening at a professional concert gives the cohort a relaxed, non-academic setting to spend time together partway through the term. A single ticketed performance with a fixed start time carries no logistics to manage on the night and works as well for a large group as a small one. A poll in the cohort Discord server drew interest from around 18–20 students — well above the 12–14 seats this proposal can cover, so a random draw among those who opt in fills the seats, as the RSVP Plan describes.

### **Event Components**

**Pre-Concert Gathering (7:00 PM – 8:00 PM):**

* The group meets in the main lobby of Roy Thomson Hall, 60 Simcoe Street, checks in together, and takes its seats before the house doors close.
* The reserved block includes any accessible or companion seating flagged on the RSVP page, so everyone sits with the group.
* Anyone who wants a casual bite beforehand coordinates a spot on the RSVP page; that meal is self-funded and is not part of this request.

**Concert (8:00 PM – 9:55 PM):**

* The group attends the performance together in the reserved Balcony-level seats.
* The concert runs about two hours with one intermission.

**Post-Concert Social (9:55 PM – 10:30 PM):**

* Anyone who wants to keep the evening going walks to a nearby café or dessert spot; this stop is optional and self-funded.

### **RSVP Plan**

* **Interest Polling:** A poll in the cohort Discord server drew interest from around 18–20 students, more than the 12–14 seats available.
* **Preference Tracking:** A Partiful event page (or a cohort Google Form) collects final opt-ins and confirms who is still committed before Tuan Khai buys the tickets.
* **Seat Allocation:** Because interest runs ahead of the seats available, a random draw among the students who opt in fills the 12–14 places, with the host included, and produces a ranked waitlist that covers any cancellation in order. Tuan Khai posts the selected names and the waitlist order in the cohort Discord.
* **Dietary Needs:** The RSVP page asks about dietary restrictions even though no food is being reimbursed; if the group arranges an informal meal before or after the concert, the organizer picks a venue with solid vegetarian and vegan options.
* **Accessibility:** The RSVP page asks about mobility and accessibility needs. Every level of Roy Thomson Hall is wheelchair accessible, accessible and companion seating and a Sennheiser assisted-listening system are available, and Tuan Khai arranges accessible seating with the box office (416-872-4255 / contactus@mh-rth.com) before the tickets are booked if any attendee needs it.
